Why is Copy or Download blocked?
At least one validation error remains. Warnings are allowed, but errors must be corrected.

          
Are dropped files uploaded?
No. Checksums, archive folders, and imported YML files are processed in your browser.

          
Why are some configuration tabs disabled?
Asset-specific tabs become available after selecting that asset or marking it as bundled.

          
What does the database popup mean?
It verifies that search is using the latest published Virtual Pinball Spreadsheet database.
